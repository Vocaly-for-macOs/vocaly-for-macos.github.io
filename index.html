Vocally: Private Voice Typing for Mac

Vocally is privacy-first push-to-talk voice typing that works in any app on your Mac. Press and hold F2, speak naturally, release—and your words appear instantly wherever your cursor is. Runs 100% locally. No audio or text ever leaves your device.

Works Everywhere

Dictate into IDEs, documents, chats, terminals, browsers—anywhere. Ideal for developers prompting AI tools, professionals handling sensitive content, or anyone typing less while staying in control.

Automatic Audio Ducking

Your music or audio lowers while you speak, then instantly returns to full volume when you stop. Seamless integration with your workflow.

Custom Vocabulary

Train it on technical terms, names, or jargon. Ensures accurate transcription for your specific needs.

Voice Commands

Configurable commands for punctuation, formatting, and more. Speak "new line" or "comma" for precise control.

Compact & Discreet

System tray interface stays out of sight but always ready. Clear visual indicator shows when listening is active.

How It Works

1. Install & Activate

Download, drag to Applications, and launch. Already fully activated—no purchases needed.

2. Set Your Key

Default is F2. Customize in preferences if preferred.

3. Press, Speak, Release

Hold the key, dictate naturally, let go. Text inserts instantly at cursor.

4. Customize & Go

Add vocabulary, commands, or tweak settings. Runs locally every time.
